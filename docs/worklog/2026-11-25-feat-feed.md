## Request

Add [SOS My Feed](https://github.com/gyedongjeon/sos-my-feed), an RSS and Atom feed reader, to
the site. Since it is not a language-practice app, also broaden the site's shared copy so it fits
it, and other apps that may join the series later.

## Changes

- **Tidying first** (`refactor:`, built site byte-identical before and after):
    - The privacy note moved from the shared `ui.privacy` to each app's `privacy`, since what stays
      on the computer differs by app (speech and history vs. subscriptions).
    - `src/data/apps.ts` gained `needsApiKey`; the app page shows the shared "Before you start"
      (AI API key) note only when it is true. True for SOS My Interview and SOS My Language.
- **App data**: a `feed` entry in `src/data/apps.ts` with `needsApiKey: false`. Its icon is the
  app's `desktop/assets/icon.svg`, copied to `src/assets/icons/feed.svg`; its screenshots are the
  app's `docs/images/feed.png` and `reader.png` on `main`, copied to
  `src/assets/screens/feed-*.png`.
- **Pages**: `src/pages/feed.astro` serves `/feed/`; `/<locale>/feed/` comes from the existing
  `[locale]/[app].astro`. The header and footer pick the app up from `apps.ts`. On an app page
  without the API key note, the privacy note spans the full width.
- **App copy, in all 19 languages**: `apps.feed` follows the app's README (`README.ko.md` for
  Korean): tagline, hook, summary, six features (one list, reading like a book, paper settings,
  Markdown download, 19 languages, self-update), privacy, platforms, and screenshot alt text.
- **Shared copy, in all 19 languages**: written to fit any app of the series rather than English
  and German practice with AI:
    - `siteDescription`, `heroTitle` ("Need rescuing?"; `heroAccent` "Send a signal." kept), and
      `heroLead` (an interview, stuck for words, too many tabs);
    - the four `common` cards: on your computer, your data stays yours, in your language (screens
      or explanations, depending on the app), free and open source (AI apps cost only the
      provider's charges).
- **Docs**: `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` list SOS My Feed and its `/feed` path.
